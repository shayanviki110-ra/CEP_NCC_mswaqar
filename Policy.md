# Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

To maintain a high standard of academic integrity across the Seneca community and provide the foundation for research, teaching, learning and working practices.

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> "A commitment, even in the face of adversity, to six fundamental values: honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility and courage."

### Key Terms

- **Cheating**: Obtaining credit for work by dishonest or deceptive means.
- **Plagiarism**: Presenting another's work as one's own without citing the source.
- **Undeclared AI use**: Using AI on an assessment without a disclosure statement.

## Policy

### Policy Sections

- **1. General**
- **2. Academic Integrity Program**
- **3. Responsibilities**
- **4. Sanctions**

### 1. General

Seneca upholds a learning community that values academic integrity, honesty, fairness, trust, respect, responsibility and courage.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

Academic integrity ensures Seneca credentials accurately reflect student knowledge and learning.

### 3. Responsibilities

- Seneca provides information and resources on academic integrity.
- Students inform faculty of circumstances affecting their performance.
- Violations can be reported following [Appendix A](#appendix-a-procedure).

### 4. Sanctions

- Sanctions are applied according to the severity of the violation.
- The student's record is consulted for aggravating or mitigating factors.

## Appendix A: Procedure

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| 1. Report the suspected violation | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, student, support staff |
| 2. Inform the student | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| 3. Collect the assessment and evidence | Within five days | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
