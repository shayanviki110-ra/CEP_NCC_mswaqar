# Classic Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

## Steps

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, mix the milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. **Tip: a few lumps are fine. Overmixing makes pancakes tough.**
4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form on the surface, about 2 minutes.
6. Cook the second side until golden. **Tip: don't press down on the pancake, or it will lose its fluffiness.**
